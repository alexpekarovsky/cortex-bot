📋 Executive Summary

On July 28, 2025, at 12:31 UTC, our security operations center detected an active ransomware attack affecting critical enterprise infrastructure. The attack began with a compromised web server and rapidly escalated to active file encryption across internal systems. This incident poses significant risk to business operations and requires immediate C-level attention and authorization for emergency response actions.

The attack vector involved a sophisticated multi-stage process: initial compromise via web shell deployment on an IIS server, lateral movement to domain systems, and deployment of ransomware that actively encrypted business-critical files. Two systems have been confirmed compromised, including one with privileged domain administrator access.
